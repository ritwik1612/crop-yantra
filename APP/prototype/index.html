Recorded data, brought together.

01

Government crop records

State, district, crop, year, season, area and production. Rice yield = production ÷ area, in tonnes per hectare.

GADM district boundaries

District names, administrative IDs and polygon vertices. Representative coordinates align observations with weather.

GADM ↗
03

NASA POWER weather

Daily temperature, rainfall, radiation, humidity, wind, pressure and evaporation proxy, summarized over fixed seasons.

NASA POWER ↗
Crop records+District locations+Seasonal weather→Rice master · 2,018 × 30

Normalize district names, apply documented historical-state matching and align weather by district/year/season. Keep rice/paddy with valid yield, coordinates and at least 60 complete weather days. No artificial observations were added. These are source-derived records, not synthetic new measurements.

Two representations of the same observations.

STANDARD

28 predictors

21 median-imputed, scaled numerical features + 7 state/season indicators. Each numerical value uses training-set statistics.

z = (x − training mean) / training std
DENOISING AUTOENCODER

16 predictors

Scaled numerical inputs → DAE → 8 latent features. Add 7 category indicators and 1 reconstruction MSE. The DAE reconstructs numerical inputs; it does not predict yield.

21 → 64 → 32 → 8 → 32 → 64 → 21
All numerical inputs and excluded columns

STATE/SEASON are encoded separately. YIELD is the target. PRODUCTION_TONNES is excluded to prevent leakage. CROP, IDREGION, DISTRICT, FYEAR and MATCH_STATUS are not predictors. Metadata stays in exported files.

DAE fitted: 180 epochs, validation reconstruction MSE 0.07724. This is not yield accuracy. Autumn/Winter were unseen during fitting and encode as all-zero season indicators.

04 / USER INPUT TO RESULT

Explain the calculation.

  1. Validate district, season, year and area

    Location alone is insufficient for prediction.

  2. Construct 21 numerical features

    Obtain seasonal weather, coordinates and state-season rice-area context. Full-season data gives retrospective estimation, not an early forecast.

  3. Reuse saved preprocessing

    Saved medians, scaling and OneHotEncoder; DAE only on the DAE route.

  4. Run a trained yield predictor

    MLP or HistGradientBoostingRegressor returns t/ha. No trained yield model is connected yet.

  5. Return yield, production and explanation

    Estimated production = predicted yield × supplied hectares. Show inputs, model/version and coverage warnings. Feature contributions need a validated explanation method; this beta does not fabricate them.

District-scale training does not establish farm-level validity. Preview yield starts at 4.20 t/ha and can be changed as a user-defined what-if assumption. Scenario controls never run a trained model. Historical yield is an observed label, not a prediction. EVLAND evaporation is not FAO-56 ET₀. Boundary coordinates are vertex averages, not area-weighted centroids. This beta does not detect diseases; its crop-health tab provides references and a photo preview only.
