# Rice reference photographs

These disease-reference assets are **not training data** and are not
evidence of a local outbreak. They remain available offline in the Windows app.

All photos: Donald Groth, Louisiana State University AgCenter, Bugwood.org,
via Wikimedia Commons. Retrieved 2026-10-18. Original files retained; displayed
with CSS cropping to fit the cards, with no generated or altered disease symptoms.

## Seven additional offline references

`references/rice-conditions-zhang-2026.webp` is the unaltered Figure 1 from:
Zhang W, Zhang T, Chen S, Li H, Zhang C and Wang S (2026),
*Wavelet-prior-guided mamba network for accurate and efficient rice disease recognition*,
Frontiers in Plant Science 17:1935930. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2026.1935930

Copyright © 2026 Zhang, Zhang, Chen, Li, Zhang and Wang.
CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The article explicitly permits reproduction with author credit and citation.
Original image: https://www.frontiersin.org/files/Articles/1935930/xml-images/fpls-17-1935930-g001.webp

The app frames the corresponding labelled panel using CSS for bacterial leaf
streak, bacterial panicle blight, dead heart, downy mildew, hispa, tungro and
normal rice. The original figure is retained unchanged, including its labels.
No lesion has been generated or retouched. Panel framing is a display change.
Labels are those of the source publication, not independent laboratory diagnoses.
Normal rice is a visual comparison, not proof that a plant has no disease.

These replace the local-only Kaggle examples. Competition photographs remain
excluded from public packaging; the app no longer references that excluded folder.

The original season-aware SVG illustrations are preserved and are shown first.
Hover previews the photograph; clicking/Enter/Space toggles and pins the view.
Photographs are not season-specific and do not change into a season-specific
diagnosis when the selected season changes.
